隐私政策

应用名称：千字图万字图大全（灵梦 - 千字图万字图大全）
开发者：OLCC TECHNOLOGY SDN. BHD.
最后更新：2026 年 8 月

本应用是一款传统文化参考梦册（字典），收录三本传统梦册共约 12,000 条梦境词条，将民俗中的梦境意象与书中记载的号码对照，供文化与研究参考。本政策说明我们如何处理你的信息。简单说：本应用以离线为主，没有账号、不需登录，我们不收集你的个人身份信息；为了运营和改进应用，我们会收集少量匿名数据（见第三节），并通过 Google AdMob 展示广告（见第五节）。

一、我们收集的信息

我们不收集、不上传、不出售你的个人身份信息（如姓名、电话、邮箱、精确位置、通讯录、联系人等）。本应用没有账号系统，无需注册或登录。

我们会收集少量与你本人无法直接关联的匿名数据（一个随机生成的安装标识、功能使用统计等），详见第三节；广告相关的数据收集见第五节。

二、保存在你设备本地的数据

以下数据仅保存在你的手机本地，不会离开设备，卸载应用即清除：

  • 你是否已确认「使用前声明」（18+）；
  • 你选择的界面语言；
  • 每日提醒的开关与时间；
  • 你的查询历史记录与连续使用天数；
  • 成功查询的累计次数与首次使用日期（用于将来在合适时机邀请你评分）；
  • 为改进离线查询效果，应用会把少量搜索关键词与其对应的传统号码缓存在本机（仅作离线匹配索引，不含身份信息、不展示；其中匿名的「关键词→号码」对应关系可能按第三节所述上传以改进共享字典）。

字典内容（号码、词条、寓意、古籍扫描图册）也内置在应用内，离线即可查询。

三、我们以匿名方式收集并上传的数据

为运营和改进应用，我们会向我们的后端服务器（托管于 Supabase）发送以下匿名数据。这些数据不含姓名、邮箱、电话、精确位置等身份信息，我们也无法据此识别你本人：

  • 匿名安装标识：应用首次启动时在你设备上随机生成的一串编号，仅用于区分不同安装、对统计与上传去重；它不是设备序列号 / IMEI / 广告标识符，卸载重装即更换；
  • 使用统计：你使用了哪些功能（例如查号、情境查询、翻书、分享）以及被查询的号码、应用版本等，用于了解哪些内容常被使用、指导完善字典；
  • 购买记录：某次购买是否成功完成（仅记录「该安装已购买」这一事实），用于解锁功能与统计；
  • 自学习映射：当 AI 情境查询成功时，应用会把「关键词 → 传统号码」这一对应关系匿名上报；当足够多的设备各自独立印证同一对应关系后，它会作为共享字典的改进项回流给所有用户（服务器会过滤掉含数字或博彩字样的内容）；
  • 意见反馈：你在「意见反馈」中主动填写并提交的文字（及你可选填写的相关号码）。请勿在反馈中填写任何个人身份信息。

应用还会向同一服务器拉取远程配置（如功能开关、公告、字典更新）。这些数据用于运营、防滥用、统计分析与改进应用，不会出售给第三方。

四、情境搜索（AI）与第三方服务

本应用提供一项可选功能「AI 关键词助手」：当你输入一段梦境描述并发起查询时，这段描述文字会通过我们的服务器中转，发送给第三方 AI 服务（Google Gemini）以提取中文检索关键词。

  • 首次使用前，应用会明确弹窗征求你的同意，并告知接收方为 Google；你可以拒绝，拒绝后应用其余功能完全可用，同意后也可随时在「设置」中撤回；
  • 我们只发送你输入的这段文字，不会附带姓名、匿名安装标识或其他任何标识信息；
  • 中转服务器不会长期保存你的文字；
  • 号码与词条始终来自本应用内置的传统字典，AI 只负责把描述转成关键词，不产生号码、不作任何预测；
  • 第三方 AI 在其服务条款下，可能将收到的输入用于改进其服务，详见 Google 隐私政策 https://policies.google.com/privacy。

如不希望发送任何内容，请勿使用该功能；其余查询均可离线完成。

五、广告与「App 跟踪透明度」

本应用通过 Google AdMob 展示横幅广告，以及一项可选的激励视频广告（观看后可获得额外的 AI 关键词助手使用额度，除此之外没有任何其他奖励）。

为投放广告，Google 及其广告合作伙伴可能收集并使用以下数据：

  • 设备标识符：在 iOS 上为「广告标识符」(IDFA)，在 Android 上为「广告 ID」；
  • 广告数据与产品交互数据：广告的展示、点击与应用内互动情况；
  • 粗略地区信息：由 IP 地址推断的国家 / 地区，用于投放当地广告；
  • 诊断数据：崩溃与性能信息。

在 iOS 上，本应用会通过系统的「App 跟踪透明度」(App Tracking Transparency, ATT) 对话框征求你的许可：

  • 若你选择「允许」，Google 可使用 IDFA 将你的数据与第三方数据关联，用于跨 App 的个性化广告——这属于 Apple 定义下的「追踪」；
  • 若你选择「要求 App 不跟踪」，则不会使用 IDFA 进行追踪，你仍会看到广告，但为非个性化广告。

你可以随时在 iOS 的「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中更改此选择。在 Android 上，你可以在系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID。

我们已在广告网络设置中屏蔽博彩与投注类广告。关于 Google 如何在其广告产品中使用数据，请见 https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

六、App 内购买

本应用提供可选的 App 内购买：一次性解锁（移除广告并提升 AI 使用额度），以及若干「添香油」赞助项目（纯属自愿赞助，不解锁任何内容与功能，也不属于慈善或宗教捐款，没有任何寺庙、慈善机构或第三方组织收取其中任何款项）。

所有付款均由 Apple App Store / Google Play 处理。我们不会收到、也不会保存你的信用卡号、账单地址或任何其他支付信息。我们仅按第三节所述记录「该安装已完成购买」这一匿名事实。

七、通知

若你开启「每日提醒」，提醒由你的设备本地定时发出，不联网、不收集任何数据。你可随时在应用内或系统设置中关闭。

八、网络与权限

本应用在以下情形会联网：使用 AI 关键词助手时（上述第四节的第三方调用）、加载广告（第五节）、发送第三节所述的匿名统计与自学习 / 反馈数据、以及拉取远程配置与字典更新。其余功能（关键词搜索、号码反查、古籍扫描图册、每日提醒）均可离线使用；无网络时这些上传会被静默跳过，不影响使用。通知权限仅用于发送每日提醒。

九、年龄限制与儿童

本应用面向 18 岁及以上成年人。应用首次启动时会要求你确认已年满 18 岁方可进入。内容仅供文化与研究参考，不面向儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。若你认为有儿童向我们提供了信息，请通过第十二节的联系方式与我们联系，我们会尽快删除。

十、你的选择与权利

  • 第三节所述数据均为匿名、无法与你本人直接关联；
  • 卸载本应用即删除全部本地数据，并停止此后任何匿名上传（匿名安装标识也随之失效）；
  • 可随时关闭每日提醒；
  • 可选择不使用 AI 关键词助手，以避免任何文字外发；已同意的也可在「设置」中撤回同意；
  • 可在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中随时撤回广告追踪许可；
  • 如需删除你已提交的反馈，或对你的匿名数据有任何疑问，可凭你的匿名安装标识联系我们（见第十二节）。

十一、本政策的变更

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更会在应用内或本页更新「最后更新」日期后生效。

Privacy Policy

App: 千字图万字图大全 (灵梦 - 千字图万字图大全)
Developer: OLCC TECHNOLOGY SDN. BHD.
Last updated: August 2026

This app is a traditional cultural reference dream dictionary containing around 12,000 entries drawn from three traditional dream almanacs, pairing folk dream imagery with the numbers recorded in those books, for cultural and research reference. This policy explains how we handle your information. In short: the app works mainly offline, has no account and no login, and we do not collect any personally identifiable information; to operate and improve the app we collect a small amount of anonymous data (Section 3), and we show ads through Google AdMob (Section 5).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, upload, or sell your personally identifiable information (such as name, phone number, email, precise location, or contacts). The app has no account system and requires no registration or login.

We do collect a small amount of anonymous data that cannot be directly linked to you (a randomly generated install identifier, feature-usage statistics, etc.) — see Section 3. Advertising-related collection is described in Section 5.

2. Data stored locally on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, never leaves the device, and is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • whether you have accepted the pre-use notice (18+);
  • your chosen interface language;
  • your daily reminder on/off setting and time;
  • your query history and consecutive-day usage count;
  • a count of successful lookups and your first-use date (used to invite you to rate the app at an appropriate time in the future);
  • to improve offline search, the app caches a small number of search keywords and their matching traditional numbers on your device (used only as a local matching index — no identifying information, never displayed; the anonymous "keyword to number" mappings may be uploaded as described in Section 3 to improve the shared dictionary).

The dictionary content (numbers, entries, meanings, scanned pages of the original books) is bundled in the app and works offline.

3. Anonymous data we collect and upload

To operate and improve the app, we send the following anonymous data to our backend server (hosted on Supabase). It contains no name, email, phone, precise location or other identifying information, and we cannot identify you from it:

  • Anonymous install identifier: a random string generated on your device the first time the app runs, used only to distinguish installs and to de-duplicate statistics and uploads. It is not a device serial, IMEI or advertising identifier, and it changes if you reinstall;
  • Usage statistics: which features you use (e.g. number lookup, scene search, picture book, share), the numbers looked up, and the app version — to understand what is used and guide improvements to the dictionary;
  • Purchase record: whether a purchase completed successfully (only the fact that this install has purchased), used to unlock features and for statistics;
  • Self-learning mappings: when an AI keyword search succeeds, the app anonymously reports the "keyword to traditional number" mapping; once enough devices independently corroborate the same mapping, it flows back to all users as a shared-dictionary improvement (the server filters out anything containing digits or gambling terms);
  • Feedback: text you voluntarily write and submit in "Feedback" (and an optional related number). Please do not include any personal identity information in feedback.

The app also fetches remote configuration (e.g. feature flags, announcements, dictionary updates) from the same server. This data is used for operation, abuse prevention, analytics and app improvement, and is not sold to third parties.

4. AI keyword helper and third parties

The app includes one optional feature, the "AI keyword helper": when you type a dream description and run a search, that text is relayed through our server to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini) to extract Chinese search keywords.

  • Before the feature is used for the first time, the app shows an explicit consent sheet that names Google as the recipient. You may decline; the rest of the app remains fully usable, and consent can be revoked at any time in Settings;
  • we send only the text you typed — no name, no anonymous install identifier, and no other identifier of any kind;
  • the relay server does not retain your text long-term;
  • numbers and entries always come from the app's built-in traditional dictionary; the AI only turns your description into keywords and never generates numbers and makes no prediction;
  • under its terms of service the third-party AI may use the input it receives to improve its services — see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you prefer not to send anything, simply do not use this feature; all other lookups work offline.

5. Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

This app shows banner ads through Google AdMob, plus an optional rewarded video ad (watching it grants extra AI keyword helper quota only — there is no other reward of any kind).

To serve ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers: the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) on iOS, or the Advertising ID on Android;
  • Advertising data and product interaction data: ad impressions, clicks and in-app interaction;
  • Coarse location: country or region inferred from IP address, used to serve local ads;
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance information.

On iOS, the app requests your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog:

  • if you choose Allow, Google may use the IDFA to link your data with third-party data for personalised cross-app advertising — this is "tracking" as Apple defines it;
  • if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not used for tracking; you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised.

You can change this choice at any time in iOS under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in system settings.

We have blocked gambling and betting ad categories in our ad network settings. For how Google uses data in its advertising products, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases: a one-time unlock (removes ads and lifts the AI quota), and several optional "oil money" tips (purely voluntary support that unlocks no content or feature; they are not charitable or religious donations, and no temple, charity, third party or organisation receives any portion of the funds).

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive and never store your card number, billing address or any other payment information. We record only the anonymous fact that this install has completed a purchase, as described in Section 3.

7. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally by your device — no internet, no data collected. You can turn it off anytime in the app or in system settings.

8. Network and permissions

The app connects to the internet to: run the AI keyword helper (the third-party call in Section 4), load ads (Section 5), send the anonymous statistics and self-learning/feedback data described in Section 3, and fetch remote configuration and dictionary updates. All other features (keyword search, reverse number lookup, scanned picture book, daily reminder) work offline; when there is no network these uploads are silently skipped and the app still works. The notification permission is used only to send the daily reminder.

9. Age restriction and children

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. On first launch it requires you to confirm that you are 18 or older before you can proceed. Its content is for cultural and research reference, is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us using Section 12 and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your choices and rights

  • the data in Section 3 is anonymous and cannot be directly linked to you;
  • uninstalling the app deletes all local data and stops any further anonymous uploads (the anonymous install identifier also ceases to exist);
  • you can turn off the daily reminder anytime;
  • you can choose not to use the AI keyword helper to avoid any text being sent out, and you can revoke consent in Settings after granting it;
  • you can withdraw advertising tracking permission at any time under iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking;
  • to delete feedback you submitted, or for any question about your anonymous data, you may contact us with your anonymous install identifier (see Section 12).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes take effect after the "Last updated" date on this page is changed.
